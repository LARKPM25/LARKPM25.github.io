Product

The path is the product.

Lead. Client. Intake. Estimate. Contract. Production. LarkPM is that walk — not a pile of tools taped together after the fact.

01

Leads arrive as leads

A form on a company website posts into LarkPM. Source, page, and contact stay on the record. Nobody retypes a name from an email.

02

Clients own the jobs

A lead becomes a client. The client owns the projects. Interactions, documents, and who on the team can see them live there.

03

Intake scopes the real job

Walk the work: deck, porch, railing, stairs. Accepting intake prices from the catalog and the calc engines.

04

The estimate is a bill of materials

Estimators bind catalog items and set quantities. Purpose-first lines. No “materials TBD” pretending to be a price.

05

Sign in the product

Dual-party e-sign. What they sign is what you scoped. Executed work files to the client documents.

06

Build on the same record

Overview, budget, receivables, approvals, dates, photos, tasks. The field and the office share one truth.
